- Working with your randomly assigned partner (A\V: Zoe\Lilith, Matteo\Mariia, KP\Ada, Bryce\Rachel), you'll have one hour to make a 3 min From Scratch LiveCoding set.

- After the hour, you'll present your work, then will submit documentation of the project and process by the end of class.

- Documentation as always reflects what you did, how you did it, problems you faced, sources you used, etc in MarkDown as a .md. Also include information about the collaboration process.
